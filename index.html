M7Methodology · Fermi estimate over ~80 countries · real solar terminator · sources

Satire built on a Fermi estimate, not a measurement. Model: idle = 1 − occupancy(local hour) × capacity-in-use, summed over ~80 countries weighted by population × device penetration × RAM per device. Local time uses each country's standard UTC offset (no DST). Night shading is the real solar terminator for the displayed time. PCs: 1.5 B × 10.5 GB. Smartphones: 6.5 B × 5.5 GB. Consoles: 300 M × 12 GB.

What “idle” means here. Powered-off, asleep, or sitting in a pocket counts as idle. Cached apps in a phone's RAM count as idle too, because nobody is using them — which is exactly the kind of accounting that makes 95% sound like a scandal. Data-center DRAM isn't 100% busy either; this page doesn't model that, but you're welcome to feel it.
